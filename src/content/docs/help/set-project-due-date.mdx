Setting a project's due date helps you adjust deadlines and ensure your project timeline aligns with current priorities and resource availability.

## How to edit the due date

<Steps>
1. Open the project page and go to the **Overview** tab
2. In the sidebar, find the **Due date** section
3. Click on the due date area (or current due date if one exists)
4. Choose a date type, then select the date
5. Click **Confirm** to save the change
</Steps>

## Date type options

The date picker offers four different granularities to match your project's timeline:

**Day** — For specific deadlines. Choose a particular day when the project should be completed.

**Month** — For broader timeframes. Useful when you need flexibility within a month.

**Quarter** — For strategic projects. Perfect for quarterly planning.

**Year** — For long-term work. Use for annual or multi-year initiatives.

## When to use each date type

**Use Day for:**
- Specific deadlines
- Projects that must finish on a particular date

**Use Month for:**
- Projects with some flexibility
- Work that can finish anytime in a given month

**Use Quarter for:**
- Strategic projects
- Work that aligns with quarterly planning

**Use Year for:**
- Long-term initiatives
- Projects that finish sometime in a given year

## When to change due dates

Consider updating a project's due date when:

- Project requirements have expanded or been reduced
- Team availability or capacity has changed
- Third-party deliverables or approvals are delayed
- Business needs have shifted the project's urgency

Keeping project due dates realistic and up-to-date ensures better planning and helps teams deliver quality work on time.
