You can change your account email while signed in. You'll need access to both your current and new inboxes. The change applies to all your companies.

<Steps>
1. Open your avatar menu and select **Password & Security**.
2. Click **Change email**.
3. Enter your **New email** and click **Send verification code**.
4. Enter the six-character code sent to your current inbox, then click **Verify current email**.
5. Enter the separate code sent to your new inbox, then click **Confirm email change**.
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot placeholder: ![Screenshot: Change email form showing the current address and New email field](./change-email.png) */}

Your old email stays active until you confirm. You'll remain signed in, and Operately will notify your previous address.

## If a code expires

Codes are valid for up to five minutes. Check your spam folder or click **Resend code**. Choose **Use a different email** to change the address, or **Start again** if your current-inbox verification has expired.

## Signing in afterward

- For email sign-in, use your new email and existing password.
- For Google sign-in, use a Google account with the new email address.

You can also [change your password](/help/change-password) from **Password & Security**.
