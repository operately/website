The **Settings** page brings together your language, timezone, time format, and theme preferences.

## Opening Settings

<Steps>
1. Click your avatar or initials in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.
</Steps>

You can also open **Settings** from the **My Account** page.

## Color Mode

Choose **Always Light**, **Always Dark**, or **Same as System**. **Same as System** follows your computer's light or dark mode setting.

See [Set appearance to light or dark mode](/help/set-light-dark-mode) for more detail.

## Language

Choose **English**, **Deutsch**, **Français**, **Español**, **Русский**, or **Português (Brasil)** for your interface and notifications.

Your language applies after saving. You choose it separately for each company's profile. It does not translate user-written content.

## Timezone

Choose the timezone used to display local times.

If you have not saved a timezone, Operately uses your browser's timezone and shows it in this field. Saving other settings keeps that browser fallback. Choosing a different timezone and saving sets your timezone preference.

## Time format

Choose **Automatic**, **12-hour clock**, or **24-hour clock**. **Automatic** follows your browser's locale.

Timezone determines which local time is shown. Time format determines how that time is displayed. Your clock format applies even when Operately uses your browser's timezone.

<Aside>
To update your name, job title, or manager, use [Profile](/help/fill-in-personal-info). To change email delivery preferences, select **Notifications** from your avatar menu. See [Notification settings overview](/help/notification-settings).
</Aside>
