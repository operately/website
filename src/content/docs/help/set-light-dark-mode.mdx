You can customize the Operately interface to match your preference for light or dark themes, or have it automatically follow your operating system's settings.

## Accessing appearance settings

<Steps>
1. Click on your avatar or initials in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu.
</Steps>

## Choosing your appearance mode

Under **Color Mode**, choose one of these options:

- **Always Light:** Sets the interface to a light background with dark text.
- **Always Dark:** Sets the interface to a dark background with light text.
- **Same as System:** Automatically switches between light and dark mode based on your computer's system settings.

Select the card for your preferred mode.

## Saving your preference

Click **Save Changes** to save and apply your chosen mode.
