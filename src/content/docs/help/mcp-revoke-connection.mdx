You can review which AI clients can access Operately through MCP and revoke a connection when you no longer want a client to use your account.

## Revoke a connection

<Steps>
1. Open your avatar menu.
2. Select **MCP Connections**.
3. Under **Connected Clients**, find the client you want to manage.
4. Open its options menu and click **Revoke**.
5. Confirm by clicking **Revoke**.
</Steps>

The page shows your active connections for the current company, including each client's access, when it connected, and when it was last used. Revoking a connection removes it from this list, invalidates its credentials, and ends its MCP sessions. The client must reconnect through OAuth before it can access Operately again.

A listed connection does not prove the client has current credentials. If a connection has stopped working, follow [Troubleshoot an MCP connection](/help/troubleshoot-mcp-connection). You do not need to revoke an active connection before signing in again from the client.
