You need edit access to the space, project, or goal where you want to create a document.

To create a new document:

<Steps>
1. Open the space where you want to create the document.
2. Click on the **Documents & Files** tool.
3. Click the **Add** button in the top left corner.
4. Select **New document**.
5. Give your document a name and add your content.
6. Click **Create document** to publish it.
</Steps>

#### Notifying people about the new document

Before you submit the document, you can choose to notify people in the 
space about the new document. By default, everyone in the space will be
notified about the new document. 

You can choose to notify specific people in the space by selecting 
**Only these people I select** and then selecting the people you want 
to notify.

Alternatively, you can choose **No one** to not notify anyone about the
new document.

## Save and find drafts

Click **Save as draft** to finish a document later. In **Docs & Files**, click **Your drafts** (with a count) to open **Your Drafts**. This list includes your drafts across folders in that space, project, or goal, with their location and last-saved time.

Open a draft to continue editing and click **Publish now** when it's ready.
