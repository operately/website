A busy pipeline and more closed deals can look like a strong month, even when revenue falls. To understand your sales results, track three sales KPIs together: **qualified pipeline created**, **deals won**, and **sales revenue**.

## What each sales KPI tells you

### Qualified pipeline created

The total value of sales opportunities that first met your qualification criteria this month. This tells you how much potential business the team added.

For example, five newly qualified opportunities worth $10,000 each add $50,000 of pipeline. Agree on what makes an opportunity qualified, and count each one once. This measures new pipeline, not the total value of all open deals.

### Deals won

How many deals the team closed successfully this month. Count each deal once, using its closed-won date. This tells you how many sales the team secured, regardless of their size.

### Sales revenue

The sales revenue recorded by your finance team for the month. Use the same reporting basis and currency each time.

Revenue can be recorded in a different period from the sale. A signed contract's full value and cash received are different measures from revenue.

## Review them every month

At the end of each month:

1. Get newly qualified pipeline and closed-won counts from your sales records.
2. Get the month's sales revenue from your finance records.
3. Log all three values in Operately.
4. Note changes in deal size, timing, or the types of customers you're reaching.
5. Decide where to focus next: finding opportunities, closing deals, or moving signed work toward delivery.

Keep your sales system for individual deals. Use Operately to review the trends with your team and agree on what to do next.
