The right KPI tracking software depends on how your team gets its numbers and what happens after someone records them. Start with three questions: **Where does the data come from? Who updates it? How will the team review it?**

## Choose the approach that fits your work

<KpiToolComparison />

### A spreadsheet for a simple shared tracker

A spreadsheet works well when you have a few KPIs, manageable updates, and someone responsible for keeping the sheet current. Start with columns for the metric, period, value, and notes. Add formulas and charts as needed.

Agree on who maintains each metric and when to review it.

### A connected dashboard for data from several sources

Consider a dashboard when copying numbers between systems takes too much time, or you need to filter results by channel, product, or region. Tools with data connectors can bring external data into charts and reports.

Before choosing one, test your actual sources, calculations, and refresh requirements. Check the cost of the connectors and access your team needs, as well as the subscription. Decide where people will record explanations and follow-up work.

### Team KPI tracking for regular review

Operately fits teams that want someone responsible for each KPI, weekly or monthly readings, and a place to discuss changes. You can log values, see their history, comment on updates, and annotate charts with events that help explain the numbers.

Collecting numbers and entering them by hand can get tedious. An AI agent with access to your data sources can gather the readings and log them in Operately through the [MCP](/help/mcp-connections) or [CLI](/help/cli).
