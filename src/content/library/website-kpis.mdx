Most teams watch website traffic, but more visitors don't always mean more conversions. To see whether your website is growing and what's driving it, track three website KPIs together: **monthly visitors**, **monthly signups**, and **conversion rate**.

## The three KPIs

### Monthly visitors

How many unique people visited your website this month. This tells you how many people you reached.

Use the visitor count from your analytics tool, not page views or sessions. One person can view many pages or come back several times.

### Monthly signups

How many visitors created an account this month. This tells you how many people took the next step.

Count completed signups only, not clicks on the signup button. Leave out invited teammates and test accounts.

### Conversion rate

The percentage of visitors who signed up:

**Conversion rate = signups ÷ visitors × 100**

For example, 300 signups from 10,000 visitors is a 3% conversion rate. This tells you whether the website is getting better or worse at turning visits into signups.

In July, traffic grew by 20%, but signups stayed at 300, so conversion dropped from 3% to 2.5%. If you looked at traffic alone, July would seem like a good month. Looking at all three KPIs shows that the extra visitors didn't sign up.

A likely reason: the team ran a broad marketing campaign in July, which may have brought in visitors who were less interested in the product. To confirm it, the team should check where the new traffic came from and make sure nothing changed on the signup page.

## Review them every month

At the end of each month:

1. Get the visitor and signup counts from your analytics tool.
2. Calculate the conversion rate.
3. Log all three values in Operately.
4. Note anything that could explain a change, like a campaign, an outage, or a tracking change.
5. Decide what to do next.

If you want to reach a specific conversion rate by a certain date, set that as a goal and keep tracking the KPIs alongside it.
