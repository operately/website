A project can have plenty of completed tasks and still miss its deadlines. To see where delivery needs attention, track three project management KPIs together: **on-time milestones**, **overdue milestones**, and **blocked tasks**.

## The three KPIs

### On-time milestones

The percentage of milestones due this week that were completed by their agreed deadlines:

**On-time milestone rate = milestones completed on time ÷ milestones due × 100**

For example, completing four of five due milestones on time gives you 80%. Include unfinished milestones in the denominator. Keep the agreed deadlines as your baseline, and note any scope or deadline changes.

### Overdue milestones

How many milestones are past their agreed deadlines and still unfinished at the end of the week. Include overdue milestones from earlier weeks. This tells you how much late work remains, even when this week's delivery improves.

### Blocked tasks

How many unfinished tasks cannot move forward at the end of the week because they are waiting on a decision, access, or other work. This tells you where the team may need help before more deadlines slip.

## Review them every week

Each Friday at the close of the working week:

1. Review the project's milestones and calculate the on-time rate.
2. Count unfinished overdue milestones and blocked tasks.
3. Log all three values in Operately.
4. Note what changed, such as a dependency, a scope change, or a decision that unblocked work.
5. Agree on who will help with the remaining delays and by when.

Check which milestones and tasks are affected before deciding what to do. One blocked task on the critical path can matter more than several small ones.
